Detective,

You spent the last little while investigating your favorite books, movies, shows, music, and stories — solving locks like Hermione, breaking codes like Walter White, and somehow talking to a capybara like it was a witness on Law & Order.

But somewhere along the way, the investigation became about something else.

Us.

Every clue was chosen for a reason. Every case was built around something you love. Every number was leading somewhere. And eventually, you found it — with only a mildly embarrassing number of hints along the way.

03/10/2026

Your birthday.

So yes... I was the suspect. I was guilty. "I am the one who knocks" — and also the one who hid your birthday behind eight increasingly ridiculous puzzles.

My motive? I wanted to give my favorite person a birthday she could actually play through — trivia, memory games, an unhelpful rodent, and all.

Happy Birthday. ❤️

And if I had to solve the mystery of who I'd want beside me for all the chapters still to come — sequels, spin-offs, and the inevitable Wildlife File 2: Capybara's Revenge — I think we both know the answer.

Case 003. Case closed. 🖤
